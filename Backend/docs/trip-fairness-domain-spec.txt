# TripFairness AI: Domain-Aligned Financial Settlement Intelligence

## 1. Domain Objective & Scope
TripFairness AI is a specialized financial intelligence layer for group travel, communal expenses, and multi-party vacation ledgers. It ensures equitable expense distribution, minimizes emotional and financial friction, detects ledger anomalies, and computes optimal minimum-cash-flow settlement transfers.

## 2. Core Mathematical Principles

### 2.1 Net Ledger Position
For every traveler i in group P:
- Total Paid: paid(i) = sum of all booking payments made upfront by traveler i.
- Total Share (Owed): share(i) = sum of all individual expense allocations assigned to traveler i across all trip items.
- Net Position: netBalance(i) = paid(i) - share(i).
- Fundamental Zero-Sum Invariant: sum(netBalance(i) for all i in P) == 0.
- If netBalance(i) > 0: Traveler is a creditor ("overpaid") and must receive funds.
- If netBalance(i) < 0: Traveler is a debtor ("underpaid") and must remit funds.

### 2.2 Spending Concentration & Gini Coefficient
The Gini coefficient G evaluates inequality of upfront cash fronting:
- G = (2 * sum(i * paid_sorted[i]) - (n + 1) * sum(paid_sorted)) / (n * sum(paid_sorted)).
- G < 0.35: Healthy, distributed upfront contribution.
- 0.35 <= G <= 0.60: Moderate concentration; rotational fronting recommended.
- G > 0.60: Severe fronting concentration where 1 or 2 travelers carry the majority of trip costs.

### 2.3 Financial Stress Index (0 to 100)
Quantifies group financial friction before it escalates:
- Fronting Concentration Risk: Ratio of top payer spend to total spend (>50% = 25 pts, >70% = 40 pts).
- Debt Saturation: Ratio of cumulative unsettled debt to total spend (>40% = 15 pts, >75% = 30 pts).
- Payment Integrity: Duplicate payments detected (+20 pts).
- Score Levels: 0-29 Nominal, 30-49 Moderate, 50-69 Elevated, 70-100 Critical.

### 2.4 Minimum Transfer Set Optimization (Greedy Min-Cash-Flow)
Direct bilateral settlements without intermediary hops:
- Sort debtors descending by absolute debt: D = [d1, d2, ...].
- Sort creditors descending by credit: C = [c1, c2, ...].
- Settle min(debt, credit) between top debtor and creditor until all balances reach 0.
- Guarantees at most (n - 1) transactions for n participants.

## 3. Anomaly & Outlier Rules
1. Statistical Outlier: Any expense with amount > mean + 2 * stdDev across trip bookings is tagged.
2. Budget Concentration: Any single expense consuming >40% of trip spend.
3. Duplicate Entries: Identical (payer, amount, title) within 24 hours.

## 4. Advisory Tone & Directives
- Provide objective, polite, and actionable recommendations.
- Emphasize transparent group harmony and early settlements.
